Several nascent products are emerging in the SaaS customer onboarding niche, with new entrants particularly focused on AI-driven onboarding agents. Here's a look at the most notable emerging products:

### AI-Powered Onboarding Agents (Newest Wave)

| Product | Backing / Status | What It Does |
|---|---|---|
| **Andoria** (YC F24) | Y Combinator Fall 2024 | An AI onboarding agent that learns how a web application works, gathers data about each user's background and goals, and generates personalized onboarding walkthroughs in real time. When a user gets stuck, the agent can step in and show them exactly what to do by performing actions in the interface [^2][^11]. |
| **OnRamp** | $15M Series A (Nov 2025, led by Koch Disruptive Technologies) | An intelligent customer onboarding and engagement platform that recently launched AI Summaries, which automatically analyze onboarding data and surface insights. The company is doubling down on AI capabilities with its new funding. |

### Customer-Facing Onboarding Portals

| Product | Backing / Status | What It Does |
|---|---|---|
| **Dock** | Self-funded / bootstrapped | Replaces chaotic email threads and spreadsheets with shared, personalized customer portals for high-touch B2B implementation projects. Built for project-based, human-led onboarding rather than self-serve SaaS [^7][^12]. |
| **Arrows** | Venture-backed (Crunchbase listed) | Creates customer-facing onboarding plans that attach directly to HubSpot deals/tickets or Salesforce opportunities/cases. Has pivoted toward AI-powered digital sales rooms that use call notes and CRM data to build personalized onboarding experiences [^4][^13]. |

### Implementation & Professional Services Platforms

| Product | Backing / Status | What It Does |
|---|---|---|
| **Rocketlane** | $18M Series A (8VC led) | Now positions itself as an "agentic PSA platform" — combining project management, client collaboration, and professional services automation. Built-in client portal, project templates, and automation. Recently added agentic AI capabilities. Pricing starts at $19/month per seat [^3][^5]. |
| **GUIDEcx** | Established | Purpose-built customer onboarding and project management platform for teams running complex, high-touch implementations [^9]. |

### In-App Adoption / Digital Adoption Platforms

| Product | Backing / Status | What It Does |
|---|---|---|
| **Userflow** | Venture-backed | Now markets itself as an "AI-powered product adoption engine." Lets teams build in-app onboarding, guide users in the moment, and analyze what to improve — all in one platform. |

### Regulatory & KYB Onboarding (Adjacent Niche)

| Product | Backing / Status | What It Does |
|---|---|---|
| **Duna** (Amsterdam) | €10.7M (2025, backed by Stripe/Adyen/Snowflake execs) | Combines regulatory compliance with user experience for business identity verification during onboarding. Already used by Plaid, Bol, and others [^6]. |
| **Uniify** (Copenhagen) | €3M (2023) | Automates financial customer onboarding, expanding from Denmark to international markets [^10]. |
| **FullCircl** | UK-based | Launched "SmartOnboard," a unified onboarding platform delivering frictionless corporate onboarding with enhanced compliance data for UK registered companies [^8]. |

### Key Trend

The clearest trend across these nascent products is the shift toward **AI agents that interact with the user's interface** — not just static guides or chatbots, but agents that can observe user behavior, learn the application, and generate personalized, real-time walkthroughs. Andoria and OnRamp represent the vanguard of this shift, while more established players like Rocketlane and Arrows are layering agentic AI on top of their existing workflow platforms [^1][^3].
